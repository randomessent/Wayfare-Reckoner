# Attribution

The data in `data/`, and the copy of it carried inside `index.html`,
is derived from three public datasets. Two are public domain. One is not,
and its credit has to travel with any copy.

## GeoNames — CC BY 4.0

`data/places.tsv` is derived from the GeoNames `cities1000` dataset.

> © GeoNames — https://www.geonames.org/
> Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
> https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Redistributing `places.tsv`, or the built app that contains it, means carrying
this credit. Nothing else is asked: the licence permits commercial use,
modification and redistribution.

## Natural Earth — public domain

`data/coastline.json`, the land mask and the region names are derived from
Natural Earth 10m `land`, `lakes` and `geography_regions_polys`.

> Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data at
> https://naturalearthdata.com

No permission is needed and no credit is required, but the project asks to be
mentioned, and it deserves to be.

## ETOPO 2022 — public domain

Elevation and local relief come from the ETOPO 2022 60-arc-second surface
model.

> NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, 2022.
> ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model.
> https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model

A work of the US federal government, and so in the public domain.
