Overland travel times · a world without roads

Reckons how long a journey takes on foot, horseback or by cart, anywhere on earth, in a world where the roads have mostly gone back to grass — finding a way over the ground rather than straight across it.

The world

At ×1 the distances are the ones on a modern map. Push it up and the same two towns sit further apart — a continent bigger than the one we have.

The journey

Towns, mountain ranges, forests, passes and parks all work, as do coordinates (40.66, -4.70). Separate waypoints with a semicolon. The route is read as a straight line, so if it ploughs over a range a traveller would skirt, name the valley towns here.
